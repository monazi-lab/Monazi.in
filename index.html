What we put help toward

Monazi doesn't run one program — it responds to what's in front of it. A person, a place, or a thing, whichever needs it most right now.

People

Families facing a sudden cost they can't absorb — a hospital bill, a month's rent, school fees due before a paycheck arrives. Direct, one-time support, given quickly.

Places

Shared spaces that fall through the cracks — a village handpump that's stopped working, a shelter roof that leaks through monsoon, a community kitchen short on supplies.

Causes

Whatever doesn't fit a category but clearly matters — an animal rescue short on feed, a relief drive after a local flood, a first set of books for a child starting school.
